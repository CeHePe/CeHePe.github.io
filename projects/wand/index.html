Project | 2024

单片机魔杖原型

Microcontroller Magic Wand Prototype

🛠️ Adafruit Circuit Playground / CircuitPython / Sensors / LEDs & Audio

一个以单片机学习为重点的交互魔杖原型，通过传感器输入、LED 和音频输出把挥动动作转化为可感知反馈。

An interactive magic-wand prototype focused on microcontroller learning, translating movement into perceivable feedback through sensor input, LEDs, and audio output.

Overview | 项目简介

项目围绕 Adafruit Circuit Playground 展开，使用 CircuitPython 读取板载传感器与加速度数据，并控制灯光和声音反馈。重点是理解单片机从输入采集、程序判断到输出响应的完整链路，而不是魔杖外形本身。

The project centers on the Adafruit Circuit Playground. CircuitPython reads onboard sensors and acceleration data, then controls light and sound feedback. The emphasis is the complete microcontroller chain from input capture and program logic to output response, rather than the wand's exterior form.

Process | 过程

开发过程先验证单片机、传感器、LED 和音频的基础行为，再把加速度变化映射为交互触发条件，并在实体外壳中完成连接与测试。USB 连接同时承担供电和程序连接两项功能，便于在设备测试时持续修改 CircuitPython 代码。

Development begins by validating the microcontroller, sensors, LEDs, and audio independently, then maps acceleration changes to interaction triggers before connection and testing in the physical shell. USB serves both as the power supply and the programming connection, allowing the CircuitPython code to be revised during device testing.
