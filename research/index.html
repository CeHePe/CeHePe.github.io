2023 · Political Text Analysis

经济危机时期的再分配辩论：基于结构主题模型的美国预算辩论研究

Debating Redistribution in Times of Economic Crisis: Examining U.S. Budgetary Debates Using Structural Topic Modeling

可查看 / View Research

研究以 2002—2011 年间 846 篇美国预算演说为材料，结合结构主题模型与双重差分考察危机、议题注意力与党派差异。结果显示，危机期间再分配议题被其他预算议题挤出；研究不直接检验政治回应性。

Using 846 U.S. budget speeches from 2002–2011, the study combines structural topic modeling and difference-in-differences to examine crises, issue attention, and partisan effects. Redistribution topics were crowded out during crises; political responsiveness was not directly tested.

2020 · Risk Modeling / CUMCM

基于风险量化、数学规划与决策树模型的中小微企业信贷策略制定

Credit Strategy Formulation for Micro, Small and Medium-sized Enterprises Based on Risk Quantification, Mathematical Planning and Decision Tree Modeling

可查看 / View Research

团队将企业数据处理、信用风险量化、决策树分类与信贷资源规划组织成一套分析流程。李欣元负责 Python 数据提取与清洗、matplotlib 和 Oracle 分析可视化，以及商业可行性评估。

The team combined business-data processing, credit-risk quantification, decision-tree classification, and credit-resource planning. Li Xinyuan contributed Python data extraction and cleaning, analysis and visualization with matplotlib and Oracle, and business-feasibility evaluation.
