Overview | 项目简介

该目录保留了一份论文 PDF 和对应封面图。由于原始写作内容以文档形式存放，这一页面的任务是为站内访问提供统一入口，并将研究成果纳入整个个人网站的展示结构中。

This folder preserves a paper PDF and its corresponding cover image. Because the original writing is stored as a document, the role of this page is to provide a unified web entry point and integrate the research output into the broader structure of the portfolio site.

Process | 过程

与以视觉迭代为主的项目页不同，这类研究页更适合作为“论文存档 + 元信息入口”。后续如果需要补充摘要、课程背景或研究问题，可以在保持当前模板的前提下继续扩展，而不需要重做整体结构。

Unlike visually iterative project pages, a research page like this works best as a combination of paper archive and metadata entry point. If you later want to add an abstract, course context, or research question, the current template can absorb that material without needing a redesign.
