Research | 2020

基于风险量化、数学规划与决策树模型的中小微企业信贷策略制定

Credit Strategy Formulation for Micro, Small and Medium-sized Enterprises Based on Risk Quantification, Mathematical Planning and Decision Tree Modeling

🛠️ Python / matplotlib / Oracle / Risk Quantification / Decision Trees

徐京伟、金霏与李欣元共同完成的 2020 全国大学生数学建模竞赛研究，以风险量化、数学规划和决策树模型支持中小微企业信贷策略制定。

A 2020 China Undergraduate Mathematical Contest in Modeling study by Xu Jingwei, Jin Fei, and Li Xinyuan, using risk quantification, mathematical planning, and decision-tree modeling to support credit strategy formulation for micro, small, and medium-sized enterprises.

Overview | 项目简介

研究面向中小微企业信贷决策问题，将企业数据处理、信用风险量化、分类模型与信贷资源规划组织为一套分析流程。目标是在模型结果与实际业务约束之间建立联系，为信贷策略提供可解释的定量依据。

The study addresses credit decisions for micro, small, and medium-sized enterprises by organizing business-data processing, credit-risk quantification, classification models, and credit-resource planning into one analytical workflow. Its goal is to connect model results with practical business constraints and provide interpretable quantitative support for a credit strategy.

Process | 过程

团队从原始企业数据中提取并清洗分析字段，使用可视化和数据库工具检查数据，再通过风险量化、决策树分类与数学规划推进信贷策略设计。李欣元作为核心成员，负责 Python 数据提取与清洗、matplotlib 和 Oracle 分析可视化，并评估方案在真实商业场景中的可行性。

The team extracted and cleaned analytical fields from the source business data, inspected the data with visualization and database tools, and advanced the credit-strategy design through risk quantification, decision-tree classification, and mathematical planning. As a core team member, Li Xinyuan handled Python-based data extraction and cleaning, analysis and visualization with matplotlib and Oracle, and evaluation of the proposal's feasibility in real business settings.
